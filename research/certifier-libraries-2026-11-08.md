The derived product boolean is TRUE only when the certifier declares hand-cut, and FALSE for every other Tier-1 declaration ΓÇö including silence. `NULL` is reserved for rows with no certifier claim (manual rows). There is no counter-category anywhere in code, data, tests, or docs.
